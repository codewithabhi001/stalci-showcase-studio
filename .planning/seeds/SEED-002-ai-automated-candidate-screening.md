# SEED-002: AI-Powered Candidate Resume Screening & Scorecard Matching

## Trigger Condition
- **Trigger:** When expanding Recruitment & Hiring Module in Milestone 2.
- **Target Phase:** Milestone 2 - HR Intelligence & AI Recruitment Automation.

## Core Objective
Integrate automated resume parsing and candidate qualification scoring to automatically rank applicants against active Job Opening requirements.

## Key Features & Deliverables
1. **Automated Resume Parsing**: Extract candidate experience, tech stack skills, education, and portfolio URLs from uploaded PDF/Word resumes.
2. **AI Match Scorecard**: Calculate a 0-100% fit score based on required skills (e.g. Flutter, Next.js, DevOps) and generate a summary recommendation for HR recruiters.
3. **1-Click AI Interview Scheduling**: Automatically trigger candidate interview invitation emails with calendar links for top-tier candidates.
